
# Web3 Protocol Research

Independent research into decentralized protocol architecture, DeFi systems, cross-chain infrastructure, and smart contract security.

## Research Focus

This repository documents technical investigations, architectural studies, and independent research into decentralized systems.

Current areas of interest:

- **Protocol Architecture:** Understanding protocol components, execution flows, and system design.
- **DeFi Systems:** Studying liquidity mechanisms, lending, exchanges, and financial primitives.
- **Cross-Chain Infrastructure:** Exploring interoperability, messaging, bridges, and trust assumptions.
- **Smart Contract Security:** Analyzing vulnerabilities, access control, state transitions, and attack surfaces.
- **Protocol Design:** Exploring new decentralized system architectures and potential improvements.

## Research Methodology

Each research project aims to follow a structured process:

1. Identify a technical problem or research question.
2. Study the relevant architecture and source code.
3. Map components, dependencies, and trust boundaries.
4. Form hypotheses and investigate them.
5. Validate findings through reproducible experiments.
6. Document evidence, limitations, and conclusions.
